**A Robust Integrated Optimal 3D Evasion and Guidance Law Against Dual Non-Cooperative Vehicles via Behavior Cloning-Enhanced RL**

**摘要.** 传统飞行器规避方法易因过度规避造成终端脱靶，而现有考虑目标打击的规避研究多局限于平面单飞行器交会场景。为此，本文建立三维四体相对运动学模型，基于极大值原理推导一体化最优规避与制导律，并通过权重系数协调双非合作飞行器规避、目标到达精度与控制能耗，并利用零控脱靶量实时估计终端到达性能。针对制导权重的在线优化问题，采用打靶法生成覆盖一定范围初始几何构型的专家轨迹，构建行为克隆增强双延迟深度确定性策略梯度（BC-TD3）框架：先利用专家数据预训练策略网络，再通过强化学习微调，以提高复杂多约束任务下的收敛速度与泛化能力。进一步地，针对角度和角速度测量噪声，设计带稀疏正则化与遗忘因子的递推最小二乘辨识方法，在线估计状态空间模型中的关键非平凡参数，降低噪声对制导指令的影响。300次蒙特卡洛仿真表明，在无噪声条件下，所提BC-TD3方法的双飞行器规避成功率和目标到达成功率分别为94.7%和94.3%，优于纯TD3及传统比例导引、程序机动方法；在测量噪声条件下，结合在线辨识后，两项成功率进一步达到96.7%和96.0%，与无辨识的纯TD3和程序机动方法成功率进一步拉大，验证了所提方法在三维双威胁场景中兼顾规避安全性、终端精度与鲁棒性方面的有效性。

Traditional evasion methods of aerial vehicles may lead to a large terminal miss due to excessive evasion maneuvers, while existing evasion studies considering target reaching are predominantly confined to planar single-vehicle encounter scenarios. To address this issue, this paper establishes a three-dimensional (3D) four-body relative kinematics model and derives an integrated optimal evasion and guidance law based on the maximum principle. This law coordinates the evasion of dual non-cooperative vehicles, target arrival accuracy, and control energy consumption through weight coefficients, while utilizing the zero-effort miss (ZEM) to estimate the terminal arrival performance in real time. Aiming at the online optimization problem of guidance weights, the shooting method is adopted to generate expert trajectories covering a certain range of initial geometric configurations, and a behavior cloning-enhanced twin delayed deep deterministic policy gradient (BC-TD3) framework is constructed. Specifically, the policy network is first pre-trained using expert data and then fine-tuned via reinforcement learning, thereby improving the convergence speed and generalization capability under complex multi-constraint tasks. Furthermore, to address angle and angular rate measurement noises, a recursive least squares (RLS) identification method with sparse regularization and a forgetting factor is designed to estimate the key nontrivial parameters in the state-space model online, mitigating the impact of noises on guidance commands. Results from 300 Monte Carlo simulations demonstrate that, under noise-free conditions, the dual-vehicle evasion success rate and the target arrival success rate of the proposed BC-TD3 method are 94.7% and 94.3%, respectively, outperforming the pure TD3 algorithm, traditional proportional navigation (PN), and programmed maneuver methods. Under measurement noise conditions, when combined with online identification, the two success rates further reach 96.7% and 96.0%, further widening the success rate gap compared to the pure TD3 without identification and programmed maneuver methods. The results indicate that the proposed method can effectively balance evasion safety, terminal accuracy, and robustness in 3D dual-threat scenarios.

# **引言**

随着非合作飞行器的不断发展，制定有效的规避策略日益关键。现有研究探索了改进比例导引律[1]、有限时间收敛制导律（FTCG）[2]、近端策略优化（PPO）[3]以及协同鲁棒视线制导律[4]，以增强针对采用纯比例导引的非合作飞行器的规避能力。然而，优先追求最大脱靶量往往导致过高的能量消耗，影响后续目标到达任务的完成。

近年来，规避过程中的能量优化受到关注。Li等人[5]提出了一种节能的PPO制导律。Zhuang等人[6]将深度强化学习应用于六自由度模型，生成了平衡姿态稳定性和能量的最优轨迹。Yao等人[7]引入了一种通过精确时序控制优化能量的FTCG律。然而，这些方法均针对单非合作飞行器场景，而实际中多飞行器协同追击更为普遍。此外，尽管Hao等人[8]研究了双非合作飞行器问题，但其采用的二维运动学模型未能捕捉真实的三维规避场景。

为弥补上述不足，本文提出了一种面向双非合作飞行器的智能三维规避算法。主要贡献包括：（1）建立了三维四体相对运动学模型，将规避与目标到达一体化问题由单非合作飞行器推广到双飞行器，并从原有研究只考虑二维运动学拓展到三维。（2）设计了同时考虑规避与目标到达的一体化最优制导律，防止过度规避导致目标到达失败，利用极大值原理推出的三维最优制导律充分考虑了规避两非合作飞行器与到达目标等多个任务间存在的耦合。（3）考虑实际应用中存在的测量误差，设计了一种带稀疏正则化与遗忘因子的最小二乘辨识算法，对状态方程矩阵进行结构化辨识。（4）利用打靶法生成考虑各类初始条件的专家轨迹，设计了BC-TD3框架，使用专家轨迹进行预填充后开启强化学习训练，提高多约束复杂任务下的收敛速度与泛化性。

# **BC-TD3 based Guidance Parameter Optimization**

本节将开发基于BC-TD3的制导参数优化框架，以解决Problem 2。首先，根据闭环制导定律的结构，连续时间参数优化问题通过单打靶法转录为有限维非线性规划（NLP）问题，并在随机抽样初始条件下离线生成满足最小规避距离和目标到达精度约束的专家轨迹。然后构建BC-TD3网络：actor通过专家演示的行为克隆预训练，随后通过双延迟深度确定性策略梯度（TD3）微调，以进一步提升泛化和闭环性能。

# **Conclusion**

This study presented an integrated three-dimensional evasive guidance framework for an evader required to avoid two non-cooperative defenders while maintaining terminal accuracy at a designated target. A four-body relative-motion model was established to describe the coupled interactions among the evader, target, and two defenders. On this basis, an optimal closed-loop guidance law was derived by incorporating dual-threat evasion requirements, target-reaching accuracy, and control-effort penalties into a unified performance index. The resulting formulation explicitly accounts for the cross-coupling effects through which a single maneuver simultaneously affects both defensive encounters and the terminal engagement.

To enable adaptive selection of the guidance weights in varying engagement geometries, a BC-TD3-based parameter optimization strategy was developed. Expert demonstrations generated by a single-shooting optimization method were used to initialize the policy, after which TD3 fine-tuning improved its adaptability in the nonlinear multi-constraint environment. In addition, a sparse forgetting-factor recursive least-squares identifier was introduced to estimate the nontrivial state-space parameters from noisy measurements, thereby reducing the sensitivity of the guidance loop to measurement errors.

Simulation results demonstrate that the proposed method achieves reliable simultaneous evasion and target arrival in three-dimensional dual-defender scenarios. In the noise-free Monte Carlo tests, BC-TD3 achieved evasion and target-arrival success rates of 94.7% and 94.3%, respectively, outperforming conventional proportional navigation, programmed sinusoidal maneuvering, and TD3 without behavior cloning. Under measurement noise, the incorporation of sparse online identification further increased these rates to 96.7% and 96.0%. These results indicate that the proposed framework provides an effective and robust solution for integrated multi-threat evasion and terminal guidance. Future work will consider more realistic vehicle dynamics, heterogeneous and maneuvering defenders, actuator constraints, and validation under broader uncertainty conditions.
